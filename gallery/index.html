ONCE UPON A STARRY NIGHT

A Tale Written in the Stars

✦

Long before our story began, people looked toward the summer sky and told a story about two stars separated by the Milky Way.

CHAPTER I

Two Different Worlds

A long time ago, there lived a celestial princess named Vega.

She lived up in the sky.

Meanwhile, there lived a mortal named Altair.

He lived down on Earth.

CHAPTER II

When Their Paths Crossed

Though they came from different worlds, somehow their paths crossed one day.

CHAPTER III

And They Chose One Another

Against the distance between their worlds, they remained devoted and in love.

CHAPTER IV

Across the Starry River

The heavens separated Vega and Altair with the great starry river we call the Milky Way.

Many thought this was the end of their love.

CHAPTER V

The Fated Day

They continue with their days alone but neither stopped thinking of the other.

Their love never faded and never wavered.

And on the seventh day of the seventh month, they were able to unite once more.

Even though it could only be for one day, they vowed to make it worth every second and to do it all again the year after.

And so, beneath the same stars,
their story seems to end there.

ONCE UPON A STARRY NIGHT

A Tale Written in the Stars

✦

Long before our story began, people looked toward the summer sky and told a story about two stars.

CHAPTER I

Two Different Worlds

A long time ago, there lived a celestial princess named Vega.

She lived up in the sky.

Meanwhile, there lived a mortal named Altair.

He lived down on Earth.

CHAPTER II

When Their Paths Crossed

Though they came from different worlds, somehow their paths crossed.

CHAPTER III

And They Chose One Another

Against the distance between their worlds, they remained devoted to one another.

CHAPTER IV

Across the Starry River

The heavens separated Vega and Altair with the great starry river we call the Milky Way.

Still, they waited for the day they could find their way back to one another.

✦

BUT OUR STORY BEGAN A LITTLE CLOSER TO EARTH

Raymond & Carolyn

We may not be a celestial princess or a cowherd, but we learned what it meant to count the days until we could be together again.

CHAPTER V

Our Story Begins

We met by chance.

What began as two people spending time together became a choice we kept making, again and again.

CHAPTER VI

No Matter the Distance

Whether separated by miles or by circumstances beyond our control, love became more than a feeling.

It became a deliberate choice to keep finding our way back to each other.

CHAPTER VII

Life Between the Stars

Somewhere along the way, the grand story became something wonderfully ordinary:

laughter, adventures, strange little moments, and a life that felt increasingly like ours.

CHAPTER VIII

A Promise

And eventually, the choice we had made so many times became a promise for all the years ahead.

THE NEXT CHAPTER

Across the Bridge Together

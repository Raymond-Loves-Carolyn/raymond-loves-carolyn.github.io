We’ve gathered some of the most common questions about our wedding day below. For a full itinerary and additional information, you can also view our Wedding Guide.

View Our Wedding Guide
01

Before the Wedding

01 What is the wedding date, time, and location?

Our wedding is Saturday, July 17, 2027. The ceremony will be held at Fairview Crystal Springs from 2:00 PM–3:30 PM, followed by our reception at HL Peninsula Pearl from 6:00 PM–10:00 PM. Visit our When & Where page for addresses, parking information, and directions.

02 What is the wedding dress code?

The wedding dress code is semi-formal, but we’d love it if you dress your very best. Guests are also welcome to wear traditional Vietnamese clothing such as Áo dài or Áo gấm.

03 Do you have a wedding registry?

Should you wish to honor us with a gift, a monetary contribution toward our future together would be deeply appreciated. We would cherish a physical gift just as much. You can find all of the details on our Registry page.

02

Wedding Day

04 Is the ceremony and reception indoors or outdoors?

The wedding ceremony will be outdoors at Fairview Crystal Springs, while the reception at HL Peninsula Pearl will be indoors. Please plan accordingly for the weather during the ceremony.

05 Where can I find parking?

Parking will be available at both venues. Fairview Crystal Springs will have designated parking for the event, while HL Peninsula Pearl has parking available at the front of the venue.

06 What can I do between the ceremony and reception?

The ceremony concludes at 3:30 PM and the reception begins at 6:00 PM. Guests are welcome to use the break to relax, check in to their hotel, grab a snack, or explore Burlingame before joining us again for the evening celebration.

03

Dinner & Reception

07 Is there a reception after the ceremony?

Yes! Our reception will be held at HL Peninsula Pearl from 6:00 PM–10:00 PM. Venue information and directions are available on our When & Where page. We look forward to celebrating with you!

08 What food and drinks will be served?

Dinner will be a multi-course Chinese banquet served family-style as well as various drinks that are alcoholic or non-alcoholic. A variety of dishes will be brought to each table throughout the evening so guests can sample different dishes. View the reception menu.

09 I have dietary restrictions or allergies. What do I do?

Please review the reception menu and let us know if allergies or dietary restrictions prevent you from eating any of the listed dishes. We’ll do our best to make sure we know about your needs ahead of the wedding.
